# PART 18 — Branding & App Names

> Status: Draft v0.1 · 2026-09-25
> **Important:** "no conflict found" below means only that **a quick web search on 2026-09-25 found no obvious existing app with that name**. It does **not** mean the name, trademark, domain or social handle is available. §18.3 lists the checks required before any name is final.

---

## 18.1 Naming criteria
1. Short (≤ 10 letters), easy to say in **Arabic and English**.
2. Football-related, with the "prediction" or "fan" idea in it.
3. Distinctive enough to register as a trademark, and not a single generic word.
4. **No betting associations** (No-Gambling Charter, Part 17). Avoid "tip", "bet", "odds", "Yalla Shoot"-style names.
5. Not tied to any club's colours or nickname, so no fan base feels excluded.
6. Works as an app icon: one letter or symbol.

## 18.3 How a name becomes final (required steps)
1. **Community vote:** put the top 3–4 names to the test league players (a Telegram poll or a question in the week-3 survey, Phase 0 kit §5.5). It's free and involves the fans.
2. **Trademark searches** by counsel (D-017):
   - Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP)
   - UAE Ministry of Economy trademark registry
   - WIPO Global Brand Database, for international filings in the relevant classes (games/entertainment software and services) **[counsel: exact classes]**
3. **Store name search:** App Store and Google Play in the KSA, UAE and Egypt storefronts, in Arabic and English.
4. **Domain + social handles:** .com / .sa / .ae where possible, plus X, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube.
5. **Native-speaker check** in Gulf, Egyptian and Maghrebi Arabic, for unintended meanings and pronunciation.
6. **File the trademark** in KSA and UAE **before** the public launch, to protect the name.

## 18.4 Shortlist & brand concepts

### A. GOOOAL / جووول (founder's choice)
- **Concept:** the joy of the goal scream, and the moment your prediction comes true.
- **Strengths:** instantly understood in every language, emotional and memorable, and it already has the founder's backing.
- **Risks:**
  - an existing, similarly named app with **betting-related features** in the same market (a brand-safety and confusion risk)
  - store-name disputes
  - "GOAL" trademark objections
- **If chosen:** a distinctive composite name reduces the risk, e.g. **"GOOOAL Fans"** / **"جووول"** as the Arabic-first wordmark. It needs counsel's trademark search and a clearly different visual identity.

### B. Mudarraj / مدرّج
- **Concept:** every fan base has its stands, and we're the stands where fans prove who knows football best.
- **Strengths:** unique, directly expresses club-vs-club rivalry, Arabic-first (our positioning), and no conflict found.
- **Risks:** English speakers may struggle to spell it. It's less self-explanatory than GOOOAL.

### C. KooraCall / كورة كول
- **Concept:** "Make your call on the koora." A bilingual name, playful and clear.
- **Strengths:** says what the app does, avoids betting words, and no conflict found.
- **Risks:** "Koora" is Gulf/Levant dialect, so it's less natural in the Maghreb. Blended names can feel less premium.

**My recommendation:**
1. Keep **GOOOAL** as the project and test-league name.
2. Put **GOOOAL, Mudarraj and KooraCall** to the community vote.
3. Have counsel search all three.
4. Choose the final name when both results are in.

If you prefer to go straight to GOOOAL, the composite form "GOOOAL Fans / جووول" is the safest version.

## 18.5 Brand platform (works with any of the shortlisted names)

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | EN: **"Predict. Compete. Represent."** · AR: **«توقّع. نافس. مثّل ناديك.»** Alternative: "Whose fans know football best?" / «جمهور مين يفهم كورة أكثر؟» *(native copywriter to finalise)* |
| **Brand personality** | The **passionate, knowledgeable friend** in your group chat: competitive but warm, proud of their club but respectful of rivals, funny but never mocking, **clean and family-friendly**, and never "gambling-cool" |
| **Voice** | Short, energetic, Gulf-friendly Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic for legal/help text). Celebrates skill ("Exact!"), not luck. Banned: all betting vocabulary (Part 17 §17.2) |
| **Visual direction** | **Night-match stadium:** a dark pitch-black/deep-navy base, floodlight glow, one electric accent colour, and bold numerals (the scoreline is the hero). Clubs appear only through our generated colour badges (D-006) |
| **Logo concept** | A wordmark with the scoreline idea: the name set in bold type. For GOOOAL, the three "O"s become a **ball-trail motion** (the scream visualised); for Mudarraj, stacked lines like stadium tiers. Arabic and English lock-ups are designed together, not translated |
| **App icon** | One bold symbol on the dark base: e.g. a **scoreboard "2–1"** or a **ball crossing a line / checkmark hybrid** ("you called it") in the accent colour. It must read at 29 pt and not resemble any club crest or bookmaker logo |

### Colour system
Every club colour belongs to someone: blue is Al Hilal, yellow is Al Nassr, green is Al Ahli, and so on. Using any of them as the main brand colour would make rival fans feel the app "belongs" to one club. So:

| Token | Proposed | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `bg/base` | Deep night navy-black (e.g. #0B1020) | App background (dark-first design) |
| `accent/primary` | **Electric lime** (e.g. #C6FF3D) | CTAs, "Exact!" moments, icon. Not a main colour of any major club in our 8 competitions **[verify]** |
| `accent/secondary` | Floodlight white-gold (e.g. #FFE9A8) | Highlights, trophies |
| `semantic/success · warning · error` | Green / amber / red, AA-contrast checked | Result states (always paired with icons and letters, Part 10 §10.5) |
| `club/*` | Generated per club (D-006) | Club badges only |
| Light mode | Off-white base, same accents (darkened for contrast) | Optional |

The hex values are placeholders for the designer, and accessibility contrast (WCAG AA) must be verified.

### Typography
- **Arabic:** a modern geometric sans with strong numerals, e.g. **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, **Cairo** or **Tajawal** (free Google Fonts).
- **Latin:** a matching geometric sans, e.g. **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
- **Scores and numerals:** a bold, tabular numeral style (one weight heavier), so scorelines line up in tables.
- Check that Arabic and Latin match in x-height and weight at small sizes. Test with real Arabic club names.

## 18.6 Competitor note added to Part 2
**Tawako3aty** is an Arabic-market prediction app on the App Store that describes points and **prizes** and competing with friends ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1468606973)). It confirms there is demand in Arabic. Its prize model is the opposite side of our No-Gambling Charter line, which is a positioning advantage for us in KSA/UAE.
